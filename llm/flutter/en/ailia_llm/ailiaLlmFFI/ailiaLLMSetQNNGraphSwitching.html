Configures QNN low-memory graph switching.

Parameters:

  • llm: LLM instance
  • enabled: 0: disabled (default), 1: enabled

Returns:

SUCCESS, INVALID_ARGUMENT, INVALID_STATE after model open, or AILIA_LLM_STATUS_UNIMPLEMENTED when enabling without QNN.

Call after Create and before opening a model. Applies to LLM, MTP, vision and audio graphs. When disabled (default), contexts are retained to prioritize speed. The LLM context is loaded with the MTP context when the MTP model is opened, or otherwise on the first generation or before the first vision or audio input. When enabled, contexts are loaded when needed and released when switching between media and the LLM to reduce memory usage, but reloading may increase processing time. In both modes, vision and audio contexts are loaded on first use. Enable this on devices with limited memory (e.g. VLM on SM8475). Disabling succeeds even when QNN is unavailable.
