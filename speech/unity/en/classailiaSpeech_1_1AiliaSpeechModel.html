◆ SetVadBufferedMode()

void ailiaSpeech.AiliaSpeechModel.SetVadBufferedMode ( bool  enable)
inline

Configures transcription at utterance boundaries detected by VAD.

Parameters
enabletrue to enable (default is false)

When enabled, the sub thread uses ailiaSpeechVadBuffered instead of ailiaSpeechBuffered and transcribes only when VAD detects the end of an utterance. Long silence of more than 30 seconds does not trigger transcription, and silence before speech is discarded. Open a VAD model with OpenVad first. Thresholds can be changed with SetSilentThreshold. Ignored in live mode (AILIA_SPEECH_FLAG_LIVE). Results can be obtained with GetResults or GetStructuredResults.
